## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept, photorealistic fluid rendering. Asset type: transparent foreground water-wave layer for an animated purified-water website hero. Create one highly realistic, crystal-clear clean fresh-water wave, isolated on a genuinely transparent alpha background. Wide landscape composition about 2:1. Side view: fluid body trailing to the left, rounded curling wave crest advancing toward the right, rising and folding in a graceful organic arc. This must look like photographed or physically rendered WATER, not a glass sculpture, not vector lines. Thin translucent water sheets, natural tiny ripples, Fresnel reflections, subtle pale cyan refraction, fine clear droplets concentrated around the crest, realistic surface tension, subtle internal caustics, luminous clean white rim highlights. Keep the water itself visibly translucent, with partially transparent interior and sharply defined wet edges, suitable for compositing over a deep navy-blue page and white headline. Premium restrained studio lighting; almost colorless water, no saturated ocean blue, no dense foam. Entire wave fits inside frame with transparent margin; no rectangular background plate, no horizon, no sea, no pool, no floor, no checkerboard painted into image, no text, no logo, no objects, no mist cloud. One coherent believable wave rather than many separate splashes.
